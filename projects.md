## **1. Twin Propulsion Mechanism for VTOL Helicopters & Drones**
A next-generation propulsion system designed to overcome the limitations of traditional helicopter rotors.

### 🔹 Key Features
- Solves dissymmetry of lift, retreating blade stall, and torque imbalance  
- Uses **twin-propelled vector-based thrust**  
- Enhanced with the **Coandă effect** for stable lift at lower RPM  
- Offers post-propulsion speeds greater than conventional fixed-wing UAVs  

### 🔹 Emergency AI System
- Capable of **autonomous landing** in pilot ejection or incapacitation scenarios  
- Uses **sensor fusion** (IMUs, gyros, GPS, vision modules)  
- Ensures safe landing in complex environments

### 🔹 Problem Solved
- Spectacle users can wear full-face helmets without removing glasses

- Sectional airbag system  
- Speed + inertia sensors for detecting accidents  
- Maintains secure fit even without a functioning chin strap  

### 🔹 Additional Capabilities

- Dynamic cavity mechanism demonstrated in prototype
